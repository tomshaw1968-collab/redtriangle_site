About Us

The Red Triangle

The Red Triangle is a licensed snooker club, pool hall and lounge bar on the rooftop of Cumbernauld town centre. We've been part of the local scene for years, giving Cumbernauld a proper place to play — whether you're in a league, knocking balls about with friends, or just after a drink while the football's on.

We're open seven days a week, with a warm welcome for members and visitors alike — you don't need to be a member to walk in and play. Families are welcome too, with supervised kids allowed until 9pm.

Across our two bars and multiple screens you'll find live sport most days, weekend entertainment, and some of the best prices in Cumbernauld. Regular tournaments run throughout the year, and Friday and Saturday nights bring karaoke and the jukebox out alongside the usual pool and snooker action.
