Snooker at The Red Triangle

12 full-size snooker tables on Cumbernauld's rooftop — open all day, every day, no membership required.

The Red Triangle is Cumbernauld's home of snooker — 12 fully recovered snooker tables on the town centre rooftop, alongside our Pool Hall, two bars, and screens showing live sport throughout the week. It's a proper club room: comfortable, well kept, and set up for players of every standard, from your first ever break-off to regular league nights. Non-members are always welcome, kids are supervised until 9pm, and the doors open at 11am daily.

Whether you're after a relaxed frame with friends, a serious practice session, or you're looking to get involved in one of our leagues, there's a table waiting for you — and membership is great value if you're going to be a regular. Pop in, rack up, and see what all the fuss is about.
