Sunday Night Handicap League

New players welcome — every event is handicapped, so players of all abilities get a fair chance to compete.

The Red Triangle Snooker League runs every Sunday night, with a full season of competitions from September 2026 through to May 2027. Whether you're an experienced league player or picking up a competitive cue for the first time, everyone is welcome — handicaps are allocated on the night, so it's never just the best players who can win.
