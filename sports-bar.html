The Lounge · Sports Bar

The Sports Bar

Two bars, big screens and Cumbernauld's best prices — live sport every day, and the weekend comes alive with karaoke, DJs and more.

Down in the heart of The Red Triangle is our Lounge Bar — Cumbernauld's spot for live sport, a good pint and a proper night out, whether you're in for the snooker or just here for the game. With two fully stocked bars, screens showing live sport throughout the week, and a lounge that comes alive every weekend, there's always something on.
