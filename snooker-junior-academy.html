Junior Snooker at The Red Triangle

Giving young players a home table — and a stage. Host venue for the Under 21’s UK Open Championship.

The Red Triangle is proud to play its part in developing the next generation of snooker players. In July 2024 we hosted the inaugural Under 21’s UK Open Championship, bringing some of the country's best young talent to our tables — and we were delighted to host the event again for the 21 & Under UK Open Championship in 2025. It's exactly the kind of event we want our rooftop to be known for: a proper stage for junior players to test themselves.

Younger players are very much part of life at the club day to day too — kids are welcome and supervised until 9pm, so parents can bring the family along for a game. If you're a junior player (or a parent of one) looking to get into snooker, get involved in club competitions, or simply want to know more about coaching and junior sessions, give us a call — we'd love to hear from you.
