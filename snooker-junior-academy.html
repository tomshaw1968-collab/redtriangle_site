Junior Snooker Academy

Free coaching for anyone under 18 — fortnightly on a Saturday, 11am–1pm.

The Red Triangle runs a Junior Snooker Academy — free coaching for anyone under the age of 18. The academy runs fortnightly on a Saturday from 11am to 1pm, and every junior who joins receives a free club membership, entitling them to half price snooker rates seven days a week. All of our coaches are WPBSA qualified and PVG certified.
