Pool League & Competitions

New players welcome — drop in for a game any time, or get involved in club pool competitions.

The Red Triangle's pool hall sits alongside our snooker floor, with American pool tables for members and visitors alike. We're proud to be one of Cumbernauld's homes for competitive pool, having hosted Cumbernauld Pool League fixtures and singles nights over the years — as well as regular in-house pool competitions for players of all standards.
