The Pool Hall

Pool at The Red Triangle

Seven Supreme Pool tables in our dedicated Pool Hall — non-members welcome, drop in any time.

Down from the snooker floor, our Pool Hall is home to seven Supreme Pool tables set up for competitive blackball play, with its own bar right alongside. Whether you're in for a casual few frames or want to get properly stuck into a game, the tables are ready and waiting — no membership required.
