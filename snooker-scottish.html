The Scottish Snooker Season

Scotland's national governing body for snooker & billiards runs a full season of ranking events right across the country.

Scottish Snooker is the National Governing Body for Snooker & Billiards in Scotland, running a full calendar of national ranking tournaments each season across several tours: the Main Tour (6 events), the Masters Tour for over-40s (5 events), the Under 21 Tour (4 events), the Junior Tour for under-15s (5 events), and the Billiards Tour (6 events).

Each tour builds towards a National Championship, with the Main Tour and Masters Tour also serving as the qualifying pathway to represent Scotland at EBSA, IBSF and WSF international events — including the World Snooker Federation World Amateur Championship. Ranking events typically run over a qualifying weekend followed by knockout stages, with seeding based on the current rankings list.

The Season

How it works

🎯

Main Tour

6 ranking events across the season, seeded from the current rankings, building to a National Championship.

🏆

Masters, U21 & Junior Tours

Dedicated tours for over-40s, under-21s and under-15s, each with their own ranking events and National Championship.

🌐

International Pathway

Top performers qualify for EBSA European, IBSF and WSF World Championship representation.
