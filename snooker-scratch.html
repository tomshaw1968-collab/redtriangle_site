Snooker Scratch League

Straight snooker, no handicaps — play it as it lies against the rest of the field.

Alongside our Sunday night handicap league, The Red Triangle also runs scratch snooker competitions — straight knockout play with no handicaps applied, for players who want to test themselves against the field on level terms. It's run alongside our regular handicap league as another way to get competitive snooker in at the club.
